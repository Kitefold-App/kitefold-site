2. The All-Spaces Task Report app

Where it runs

The app is built on Atlassian Forge. It runs on Atlassian's cloud platform, inside the same Atlassian cloud as your Confluence site. Kitefold has no servers of its own, and the app makes no calls to any service outside Atlassian. Your organisation's Confluence content stays under your organisation's control and Atlassian's terms; see Atlassian's privacy policy.

What it reads

To show the report, the app reads from Confluence: tasks (their text, status, due date and assignee), the titles and links of the pages and blog posts they're on, and space names and keys. People are shown by their Atlassian account, using Confluence's own name and picture.

The report reads all of this as the person viewing it, so Confluence only returns what that person is allowed to see. It's used only to display the report on screen and is not saved.

What it changes

  • Ticking a task in the report changes that task's status (done or not done), as the person who ticked it. Confluence refuses the change if they can't edit the page.
  • The weekly digest, once someone turns it on, creates a new Confluence page each week under the page that holds the digest. That page lists each person with tasks overdue or due in the next 7 days, with an @mention and two numbers. It contains no task text and no page titles. Confluence notifies the people mentioned in the usual way. Anyone who can view the digest's page can see the names and numbers on it.

How the digest counts tasks

The digest runs on a schedule when nobody is viewing, so it uses Forge's ability to act on behalf of a user. It reads tasks and spaces first as the person who last turned the digest on or saved its settings, then as each person it lists, so every number is what that person could see for themselves. This is only used for reading tasks and spaces. The digest page itself is created by the app.

What it stores

The app stores no task data. For each weekly digest, it keeps these settings in Atlassian's Forge storage:

  • the page the digest belongs to, the spaces it counts and the day it posts
  • whether it's on
  • the Atlassian account ID of the person who last turned it on or saved its settings (so the digest can read tasks as them)
  • the title, link and time of the last digest posted, and the last error, if any

Pressing Turn off deletes a digest's settings. When the app is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the app's stored data under its own retention rules for Forge apps (it can be restored if the app is reinstalled within 21 days).

Logs

Like all Forge apps, the app writes technical logs that Atlassian keeps for a limited time and that Kitefold can read to fix problems. Our logs record things like how many people a digest counted and error codes. They are designed not to include task text or page content.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't send your data outside Atlassian.
  • It doesn't use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • It doesn't send your content to AI services.
  • It doesn't sell or share your data.

3. Licences and purchases

The app is sold through the Atlassian Marketplace, and Atlassian handles all payments. We never see card or bank details. Atlassian shares licence information with us, which can include your organisation's name, your Confluence site address, and the names and email addresses of your technical and billing contacts. We use it only to manage licences, provide support and send important notices about the app. We keep it while your licence is active and afterwards for as long as the law requires us to keep business records (usually up to 6 years).

4. Support emails

If you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you send, and use it only to help you. We keep support emails for up to 2 years after the conversation ends, unless they're part of a business record we must keep for longer. Please don't send passwords, API tokens or confidential content.

5. This website

kitefold.app sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking, adverts, embedded videos, external fonts or third-party scripts. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security and to run the service; see GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive or use those logs.

6. Why we're allowed to use data

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests: to provide the app's features your organisation chose to use, to fix problems, and to keep this website working and secure.
  • Contract: to manage licences and give support.
  • Legal obligation: to keep business and tax records.

7. Who else is involved

  • Atlassian hosts and runs the app, handles Marketplace purchases and processes your Confluence data under its own terms.
  • Hostinger provides our email service, so support emails are stored with them.
  • GitHub hosts this website.

These providers may process data outside the UK. They use their own legal safeguards for international transfers. We don't share personal data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

8. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, and to ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it or stop using it. Email hello@kitefold.app and we'll reply within one month.

Your Confluence content belongs to your organisation, so for data inside your Confluence site (such as tasks or digest pages), please contact your organisation's Confluence admin first.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We'd appreciate the chance to put things right first.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app or this website starts using data differently, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Marketplace listing.
