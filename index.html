How Kitefold apps are built

These rules apply to every Kitefold app, and you can check them in each app's Marketplace listing and privacy details.

Runs on Atlassian Forge

The apps are hosted by Atlassian, inside your Atlassian cloud. There are no Kitefold servers, and the apps make no calls to outside services.

Shows only what you can see

The apps read Confluence as the person viewing them, so Confluence's own permissions decide what appears. Nobody sees content from a space or page they can't open.

Keeps as little as possible

Your pages and tasks are read when needed and never copied into storage. No analytics, no tracking and no AI services see your content.
