Support and service levels

Last updated 1 November 2026

How to get help with Kitefold's apps, when we're available and how quickly we reply.

In short: email support@kitefold.app. We're available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 UK time. You'll get a first reply within one working day, and the same working day for urgent problems.

1. Which apps this covers

These service levels cover every Kitefold app for Confluence Cloud on the Atlassian Marketplace, on free and paid licences alike:

  • All-Spaces Task Report (help)
  • Link Health & Cleanup (help)
  • Query & Bulk Actions (help)

Each app's help page answers the most common questions, so it's worth a quick look first.

2. How to get help

Email support@kitefold.app. Support is in English. It helps to include:

  • the app's name and your Confluence site address (for example yourcompany.atlassian.net)
  • the page, tab or search you were using
  • what you expected and what happened, with a screenshot if you can
  • whether it stops people working (see urgent problems)

Please don't send passwords, API tokens or confidential page content. We'll never ask for them.

3. Support hours

DaysMonday to Friday
Hours9:00 to 17:00
Time zoneUK time (Europe/London: GMT in winter, BST in summer)
ClosedWeekends and UK bank holidays (England and Wales)

A “working day” below means a day within these hours. You can email at any time; messages that arrive outside these hours are picked up from the start of the next working day.

4. Response times

These are targets for our first reply from a person (not an automatic acknowledgement).

PriorityWhat it coversFirst reply
UrgentThe app doesn't work for anyone on your site; something the app did changed the wrong content; or you think data may be exposedThe same working day. If it arrives after 15:00, by 12:00 the next working day
NormalEverything else: a feature not working as expected, a question, a licence query, a feature requestWithin one working day

Please put URGENT at the start of the subject line for urgent problems, so we see them straight away.

5. After the first reply

  • We'll tell you what we've found and what happens next: a workaround, a fix in a new version of the app, or why something works the way it does.
  • Fixes are released through the Atlassian Marketplace and reach your site automatically. How long a fix takes depends on the problem, so we don't promise a fix time, but we'll keep you updated until it's sorted.
  • Many problems come from Confluence or Atlassian's platform rather than the app. If so, we'll say so and, where we can, raise it with Atlassian.
  • Feature requests are welcome. We read every one, and they shape what we build next.

6. Security issues

If you think you've found a security problem in a Kitefold app, email support@kitefold.app with SECURITY in the subject. It's treated as urgent. Security fixes follow the timelines in Atlassian's Marketplace security bug fix policy. How we handle your data is set out in our privacy policy.

7. Changes to these service levels

If our hours or response times change, we'll update this page and the date at the top. This page describes how we support our apps; it doesn't change the terms you accepted when you installed an app from the Atlassian Marketplace.
