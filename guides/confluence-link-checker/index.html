Free Confluence link checker

Paste a Confluence Cloud page here to see its links that go nowhere: links to pages that don't exist, links still pointing at your old Data Center or Server address, links to other Confluence sites, and addresses that only work inside your office network.

Private by design: the checker runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or sent anywhere, and this page makes no network requests of its own. Close the tab and it's gone.

How to use it

  1. Open the Confluence page in view mode (not the editor).
  2. Select the page's text: drag across it, or click in it and press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on a Mac). Copy it with Ctrl+C.
  3. Paste it into the box below and press Check links. You can paste several pages one after another, or a plain list of web addresses, one per line.

What it checks

  • Links to a page that doesn't exist. In Confluence these look like any other link, but clicking one starts creating a new page instead. The checker spots them from the address Confluence gives them.
  • Links to your old server. Links to the address you enter above, and any link to another site whose address looks like a Confluence page (/display/, viewpage.action, /download/attachments/, short /x/ links). These stop working once the old server is switched off.
  • Links to a different Confluence Cloud site, such as a sandbox or an old site from before a move. Your own site is worked out from the links you paste.
  • Internal-only addresses: localhost, private network addresses (10.…, 192.168.…), names without a dot, file:// links and shared-drive paths. They only work for people inside your network, or on one computer.
  • Worth a look: old-style Confluence addresses on your own site, which usually still redirect, and plain http:// links.

What it can't check

  • Whether outside websites still work. Browsers don't let one website test another's links, so the checker lists outside links without testing them. Open any that matter.
  • Missing files. A link to an attachment that's been deleted usually doesn't show when you view the page, so it isn't in what you copy.
  • More than the pages you paste. It checks one page, or a few, at a time. It can't see which pages link to which, or find orphan pages and unused attachments.

Every page at once, with Link Health & Cleanup

Link Health & Cleanup does these checks across every page and space on your site without copying and pasting. It keeps an index of every link, and lists broken links (missing pages and missing files), links to your old server, orphan pages and unused attachments. Every list only shows pages the viewer can open, and you can export any list to CSV.
