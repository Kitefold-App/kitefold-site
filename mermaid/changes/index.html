First release: coming to the Atlassian Marketplace

Mermaid 12.3.0. The first release includes:

  • Paste anything. Code fences, chat text, smart quotes and stray characters are removed; only the diagram is drawn and saved.
  • Sharp, full-width diagrams that follow Confluence's light and dark themes, with a per-diagram override, drawn in Atlassian's colours.
  • Full screen over the whole window with zoom and pan, for every reader.
  • A live preview in the settings window, with line numbers and plain-words errors that name the line and keep the last good drawing.
  • Edit on the diagram for flowcharts: rename steps and labels, connect, add and remove steps, undo, all written back into the text.
  • Ten templates and four plain-text converters (outline, arrows, labels, tasks), with no AI.
  • PNG and SVG download from the page and from full screen; PDF and Word export show the diagram as a picture.
  • Busy pages that settle quickly: each diagram takes its full height the moment Confluence starts its frame, and a drawing never moves the page again.
  • Free, no account, nothing leaves Atlassian. The text stays in your pages; the app stores nothing about your diagrams or your people (privacy policy).
