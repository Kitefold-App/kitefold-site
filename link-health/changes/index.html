What's new

Changes to Link Health & Cleanup, newest first.

3 November 2026: small update

  • Leave a review. The admin page's footer now links to the app's Marketplace reviews.
  • Also by Kitefold. The footer link to All-Spaces Task Report now opens its Marketplace listing.
  • Security updates to three software libraries the app uses. Nothing changes in how the app works.

2 November 2026: now on the Atlassian Marketplace

The first public release. It includes:

  • Broken links. Links to pages, blog posts, spaces or files that don't exist or are in the trash, across every space.
  • Old server links. Every link that still points to your old Data Center or Server address.
  • Orphan pages. Pages no other page links to, with Archive selected to tidy them away (they can be restored).
  • Unused attachments. Files no page shows or links to, largest first, with Move selected to trash.
  • Stale pages. Pages nobody has edited for 1 to 24 months.
  • Incoming links on every page. “Linked from 12 pages” under each title, and the Incoming links macro.
  • CSV export of every list, and an Overview with each space's numbers.
  • Checked as you. Every list and count only includes pages you're allowed to open. No page text is stored, and nothing is deleted permanently (privacy policy).
